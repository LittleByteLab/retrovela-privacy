Retrovela Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

App: Retrovela: GBA Emulator (Android package: com.littlebytelab.retrovela.gba)

Developer and privacy contact: LittleByte Labs — littlebyte.lab@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy describes Retrovela 1.0 and this privacy policy website. LittleByte Labs is the developer name; it does not imply a particular incorporated legal entity. Retrovela is a free GBA emulator for compatible games you supply and have the right to use. No commercial ROMs are bundled.

2. Information processed on your device

Retrovela processes selected game files and their SHA-256 hashes to identify games, avoid duplicates and associate saved progress. It stores battery saves, three save-state slots and protective save backups in app-managed storage. It also stores game display names, original filenames, cover thumbnails, recent-play times, authorized folder references, touch-control layouts, controller mappings and preferences such as language, volume and display mode.

Controller vendor/product information and an input-device descriptor are used locally to distinguish controller profiles. They are not used as advertising identifiers. Local diagnostic logs may contain file paths, controller information, input events, errors and performance measurements.

Retrovela 1.0 has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDK or automatic crash-report upload. It does not actively send your ROMs, saves, library, preferences or diagnostic logs to LittleByte Labs. We do not operate an in-app cloud game/save service, sell your information or use it for targeted advertising. Local processing does not mean that files can never leave your device through Android or services you choose.

3. Files, permissions and user-initiated transfers

You select ROMs, folders, cover images and save import/export locations through Android's system file picker. Selected game files are copied into app-managed storage; choosing an original file does not give the developer access to it. Covers are copied into app storage as size-limited thumbnails. The system retains the document permissions you grant.

A file provider you choose may be a third-party cloud service. Opening a cloud file may cause that provider to download it, and exporting a save to a cloud location may cause the provider to upload it. These actions are initiated by your selection and are governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy. Exporting the bundled open-source archive exports fixed application source materials, not your ROMs, saves or logs.

Game screenshots are saved to the system image collection under Pictures/Retrovela. Android 9 and earlier may request storage permission for this operation; newer versions use MediaStore. This version does not request Internet, broad all-files management, location, camera, microphone, advertising-ID or Bluetooth-scanning permissions. Gamepad input is received through Android's input system.

4. Android backup, device transfer and photo synchronization

The app does not explicitly disable Android system backup. Depending on your device, backup settings, backup service and storage limits, Android or the device manufacturer may back up app files and preferences or transfer them to another device. Your gallery or document provider may also synchronize screenshots or exported files independently.

These system/provider activities can transfer data off the device even though Retrovela does not itself upload it to a developer server. LittleByte Labs does not gain access to your system backups through the app. Review the relevant Android, device-manufacturer, gallery and cloud-provider settings and privacy policies to manage these activities.

5. Retention, deletion and your controls

App-managed game files, progress, metadata and settings generally remain until changed through available controls or removed by Android's storage lifecycle. You can edit game display names, remove custom covers and change supported settings. You can manage or revoke file access through Android or the relevant file provider. Revoking access to an original source does not delete a copy already imported into app storage.

The current version does not provide an account or a developer-operated remote deletion service. To remove all app-private data, Android offers Clear storage and uninstall controls. These are destructive and may remove imported games, saves, metadata, settings and authorizations; export any progress you want to keep first. Removing only the original ROM does not necessarily remove imported copies or associated saves. We do not claim that this version provides a per-game library deletion control.

Exported saves, screenshots, original files and copies held by other apps may remain after app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Delete those separately using the appropriate file, photo or cloud service. System backups and device-transfer copies follow the relevant provider's retention and deletion rules; clearing app data does not guarantee that every backup is deleted.

6. Information you choose to send to support

If you email littlebyte.lab@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to investigate the issue, reply and handle related privacy requests. Do not send commercial ROMs, passwords, identity documents or unrelated private files. Review logs and remove private filenames or paths before sending them.

Support correspondence is handled through our email service provider under its own privacy practices. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records or meet applicable legal obligations, then delete it subject to the email provider's backup lifecycle. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email; we will assess the request and explain any applicable retention requirement. This is not a promise that we can remotely delete data stored on your device or with your chosen providers.

7. Privacy policy website

This site consists of static pages. Our page code adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, remote fonts or tracking scripts. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses and technical connection information, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Reading the website is separate from the app's offline operation. Emailing us opens your email service and is subject to that service's practices.

GitHub General Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

8. Security and children

We use Android app storage and permission mechanisms for local data. No storage or communication method is completely secure. Only import or export files and use providers you trust. Retrovela does not require age, identity or account information to run games and does not intentionally solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and handle it appropriately. This statement does not determine the app's store age rating.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate app or store notices when needed. For questions or privacy requests, contact LittleByte Labs at littlebyte.lab@gmail.com.
